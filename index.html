RESEARCH & UNIVERSITY TEACHING

Lina Xu 徐丽娜

PhD Candidate in Computational Media and Arts

The Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (Guangzhou)

I am a PhD candidate in Computational Media and Arts at The Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (Guangzhou), expecting to graduate in 2027. I hold an MA in Interaction Design Communication from University of the Arts London, with an earlier background in visual communication design. Alongside my research, I bring over two years of full-time university lecturing experience, independently teaching and assessing undergraduate modules in UI Design, App Design and New Media Web Design.

My research explores how colour and visual environments shape cognition, behaviour and experience. Working across colour cognition, neuroergonomics and physiological computing, I combine controlled experiments, fNIRS, eye tracking and interpretable multimodal modelling to understand the relationships between visual design, cognitive performance and physiological responses. I am extending this work towards evidence-informed, cognition-aware, personalised and adaptive human–AI interaction.

01 / RESEARCH

Colour, Cognition &
Human-Centred Technologies

My interdisciplinary research connects colour cognition, human factors, neuroergonomics and physiological computing to understand how visual environments shape cognition, behaviour and experience. I use controlled experiments, fNIRS, eye tracking and interpretable multimodal modelling to inform human-centred design.

I am extending this work towards human-centred AI, personalised and adaptive interaction, and neuroadaptive interfaces that could respond to task demands, cognitive states and individual needs.

02 / TEACHING

University Teaching in
Interaction & Visual Design

I bring over two years of full-time university lecturing experience, independently teaching and assessing UI Design, App Design and New Media Web Design. My teaching connects visual foundations with user research, interaction flows and prototyping, guiding students from initial concepts to resolved digital outcomes.

At HKUST (Guangzhou), I have also served as a Teaching Assistant for postgraduate research-methods teaching, delivering a lecture on qualitative, quantitative and mixed methods, facilitating paper discussions, and contributing to assessment and feedback.
