RESEARCH & UNIVERSITY TEACHING

Lina Xu 徐丽娜

PhD Candidate in Computational Media and Arts

The Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (Guangzhou)

I am a PhD candidate in Computational Media and Arts at the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (Guangzhou). I hold an MA in Interaction Design Communication from the University of the Arts London. Before my PhD, I spent over two years as a full-time university lecturer, independently teaching undergraduate courses in digital and interaction design.

My work bridges design, cognitive science and multimodal physiological computing, with growing interests in personalised and adaptive technologies and human–AI interaction.

01 / RESEARCH

Colour, Cognition &
Adaptive Interaction

I investigate how visual environments, particularly colour, influence cognitive performance, behaviour and physiological responses. My research combines controlled experiments, fNIRS, eye tracking and interpretable multimodal analysis, while also exploring EEG and EDA as complementary physiological measures. I examine how these effects vary across cognitive tasks and individuals, and their implications for personalised and adaptive interaction.
