RESEARCH & UNIVERSITY TEACHING

Lina Xu 徐丽娜

PhD Candidate in Computational Media and Arts

The Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (Guangzhou)

I am a PhD candidate in Computational Media and Arts at the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (Guangzhou), supervised by Prof. Luwen Yu and Prof. Yuyang Wang. I hold an MA in Interaction Design Communication from the University of the Arts London. Before my PhD, I spent over two years as a full-time university lecturer, independently teaching undergraduate courses in interaction design.

My research lies at the intersection of colour cognition, multimodal physiological computing and human–computer interaction. I investigate how visual environments influence cognitive performance, behaviour and physiological responses through controlled experiments, fNIRS, EEG, eye tracking, EDA and interpretable data analysis. Building on this work, I am exploring personalised and adaptive interfaces that respond to individual differences and changing cognitive states.

01 / RESEARCH

Colour, Cognition &
Adaptive Interaction

My interdisciplinary research connects colour cognition, human factors, neuroergonomics and multimodal physiological computing. Through controlled experiments and interpretable analysis, I examine how visual environments shape cognitive performance, behaviour and physiological responses, drawing on fNIRS, EEG, eye tracking and EDA.

I am exploring personalised and adaptive interfaces, neuroadaptive interaction and human–AI interaction informed by individual differences, task demands and changing cognitive states.

02 / TEACHING

University Teaching in
Interaction & Visual Design

I bring over two years of full-time university lecturing experience, independently teaching and assessing UI Design, App Design and New Media Web Design. My teaching connects visual foundations with user research, interaction flows and prototyping, guiding students from initial concepts to resolved digital outcomes.

At HKUST (Guangzhou), I have also served as a Teaching Assistant for postgraduate research-methods teaching, delivering a lecture on qualitative, quantitative and mixed methods, facilitating paper discussions, and contributing to assessment and feedback.
